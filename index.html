Inncon Group · Engineering lead for mydatasbd

  • Manage three in-house engineers and three vendor companies. Assign their tasks and review the work they deliver.
  • Built the platform's new digital wallet. The old one had too many calculation errors, so we replaced it instead of fixing it. The new wallet uses double-entry accounting, so every balance comes from journal and ledger entries instead of a stored number, and any amount can be traced back and checked.
  • Automated the monthly profit share. The system calculates each partner's profit and adds it to their wallet directly. Over BDT 40 million goes through this process every month.
  • Built partner-to-partner transfer and withdrawal on the same ledger. For a withdrawal, the system creates a payout list. The accounts team then pays by cheque, or sends the list to the bank for payment. No third-party payment service is used.
  • Built a scheduled job that moves a set percentage from a partner's main wallet to their shopping wallet on a fixed date each month. It posts to the same journal and ledger, so both balances stay correct.
  • Built the digital deed system. It creates the investment deed for each partner online and prepares it for printing on BDT 100 judicial stamp paper.
  • Added the shopping wallet as a payment method in the online store, so partners can pay from their wallet balance instead of an outside payment gateway.
